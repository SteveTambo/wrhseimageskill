# WRHSE product-card style: measurable rules

Derived from the reference cards in `cards/`. Use these to describe the
treatment in words when an image tool cannot take reference images, and to
check a result.

## Canvas and framing

- **Portrait 2:3** (the references are 600 x 900 units; deliver 1200 x 1800 px
  or 800 x 1200 px).
- WRHSE product cards crop images to a tall frame, so **only the central 75% of
  the width is always visible**. Keep the whole product, and any text on it,
  inside that central band, with at least 12% of the width clear on each side.
- One product, centered horizontally, resting low on the canvas: its base and
  grounding shadow sit about **75% of the way down**, and the tallest items
  start about a quarter of the way down. That leaves roughly a quarter of the
  canvas empty above and below. Do not fill the frame edge to edge.

## Background

- A flat **vertical gradient**: a very light tint at the top moving to a
  slightly deeper pastel at the bottom (for example `#eef6ef` to `#cfe5d3`,
  `#fff6e0` to `#ffe3a8`, `#eef2ff` to `#cfd8ff`).
- Hue is a soft pastel chosen to complement the product. No patterns, scenes,
  props, horizon lines or textures.
- Optional: a single faint, pale circle high on one side, like a soft
  highlight. Never more than one.

## The product

- Flat, vector-like shapes: solid fills, a few simple shades, confident clean
  edges. A thin darker outline in a color close to the fill, around 3-4 px at
  600 units wide.
- No photographic texture, noise, glare, reflections or dramatic lighting.
- Keep real proportions, parts and colors. Simplify rendering, never the
  design.

## Shadow

- One soft, flat **ellipse** centered under the product at about 75% down:
  black at 13-14% opacity, roughly 57% of the canvas width and 5-6% of its
  height. No cast shadows, no reflections.

## Text on products

- Keep legible text, logos and marks exactly as they appear on the source. If
  they cannot be kept accurately, do not redraw them: choose the conservative
  cleaned-photo treatment instead.
- The reference cards' own words are placeholders. Never reuse them.

## Quick check before showing a result

- [ ] 2:3 portrait, product fully inside the central 75% width
- [ ] One product, same count and parts as the source
- [ ] Gradient pastel background, no props, no text added
- [ ] One soft ellipse shadow
- [ ] Colors and any printed text match the source
- [ ] Nothing hides damage or wear
