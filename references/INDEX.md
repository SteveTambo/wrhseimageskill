# Reference cards

Approved WRHSE product-card artwork. Use these **only for visual treatment**:
background, outline weight, shading, shadow, framing and overall friendliness.
The merchant's photo is the authority for the product itself.

Never copy these cards' text (the placeholder labels such as `SHEA`, `COLA`,
`GALAXY WARS`), brands, characters or objects into a merchant's image.

All files are 800 x 1200 PNG (2:3 portrait), rendered from WRHSE's original
demo-store illustrations. Pick **two or three** that fit the product; more than
that tends to blur the style.

## Choosing references

1. Match the **shape** first (wide, tall, flat, translucent, many-part), then
   think about color.
2. Choose a reference whose background hue sits near, but is not identical to,
   the product's own color. A green product on a green card disappears.
3. If nothing fits, use `cards/beauty-botanica-butter.png` and
   `cards/food-nachos.png` as a safe general pair.

## Limits of this set

- The references are deliberately light on printed text. For a text-heavy
  product (packaging with lots of small print), keep the text from the photo or
  use the cleaned-photo treatment; do not redraw it in the illustrated style.
- There is no headwear, footwear, or jewelry card yet. For those, take the shape
  from the merchant's photo and the style from the closest card above.
